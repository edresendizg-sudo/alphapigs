01 — THE PIGS

One pig. 4,100 cubic feet.

A storage pig is a portable silo that keeps dry bulk sealed, dry and ready on site. Built low, towed in and ready for the next job.

FIG.01TOWABLE

Horizontal Storage Silo (The "AlphaPig")

Built for structural integrity and maximum material performance. Engineered using heavy-gauge, high-tensile steel and premium pneumatic components, our horizontal silo delivers dependable bulk storage that protects your material and optimizes your terminal footprint. It offers a premium, high-capacity solution engineered to withstand heavy, continuous pneumatic cycles where overhead clearance is restricted.

  • +Industrial-Grade Construction: Reinforced structural steel tank shell built for maximum service life and high-volume cycles.
  • +Advanced Fluidization Technology: Engineered hopper aeration system maximizes material flowability and eliminates product bridging.
  • +High-Efficiency Pneumatic Transfer: High-capacity manifold line system ensures balanced loading and rapid, zero-waste discharge.
  • +Optimized Low-Profile Engineering: Maximum cubic capacity packed into a space-saving footprint to eliminate overhead clearance constraints.

ALPHAPIGS · AP-4100REV. A
01
Nominal Capacity
4,100 cubic feet (+ or - 3%)
02
Compartments
Single (1) compartment design
Capacity & Structural Limits
03
Recommended Working Capacity Range
170 to 190 US Tons. Due to material fluidization, pneumatic expansion ("fluffing"), and bulk density variances (e.g., standard Portland cement, lime, fly ash), the functional volumetric capacity typically fills within this range.
04
Overloading Consequences
Loading material in excess voids all warranties. Overloading subjects the landing gear, support structures, and internal reinforcement rings to extreme fatigue, risking unrecoverable metal deformation (shell buckling), weld fractures, or complete structural collapse.
Tank Shell & Structural Engineering
05
Shell Material
3/16" gauge steel reinforced with internal structural rings.
06
⚠️ Recommended Unit
A 1/4" heavy-duty steel shell is recommended for Barite to handle extreme material density and pneumatic abrasion.
07
Design Pressure
15 PSI operating pressure.
08
Manholes
One (1) 20" full-opening hatch strategically located at the dead center.
Aeration & Discharge Specs
09
Discharge Cones
Six (6) 45-degree hoppers angled for high-density flowable powders.
10
Aeration System
Built using 30" cotton pad aeration systems rather than fluidizing rubber pads, which some operators prefer for maximum air distribution across dry cement and lime.
11
Discharge Piping
5" Schedule 40 steel pipe outfitted with 6" butterfly valves at each individual hopper.
12
Air Control lines
4" steel piping on the curbside, managed via a 4" main butterfly valve and dual 4" check valves.
13
Blow-Down Infrastructure
A dedicated 4" steel blow-down line situated at the rear of the silo.
14
Fill Intake Lines
Four (4) separate 4" steel fill lines (two positioned on the roadside and two on the curbside) for balanced manifold loading.
Pressure & Safety Monitoring
15
Gauges
Dual system pressure gauges mounted on the roadside for operator-level visibility during pneumatic transfer cycles.

Note: This silo is not designed to transport material. It can only be moved empty.
